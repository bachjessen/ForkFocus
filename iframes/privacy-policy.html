Privacy Notice

SocialFocus works locally by default.
You can use SocialFocus without an account in Local Mode. Optional cloud features may transmit your email address, user unique ID, authentication data, and extension settings only if you choose to use or enable them.

Local Mode (default)

When using SocialFocus without an account, website modification features, focus tools, and settings work locally in your browser.
No personal data is transmitted to our servers in Local Mode.

Optional Cloud Features

If you choose to use any of these optional features:

  • Account / Sync - May transmit your email address, user unique ID, authentication data, and extension settings to enable account access and cross-device sync.

Data Transmission Summary

  • Email address - Used for account login and account-related features.
  • User unique ID - Used to identify your account and sync your settings across devices.
  • Extension settings - Used to restore your selected SocialFocus options on your devices.
  • Authentication data - Used to keep you signed in securely.

All transfers are encrypted using HTTPS and occur only if you choose to use or enable optional cloud features.
